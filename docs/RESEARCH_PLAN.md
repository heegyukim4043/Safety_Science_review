**Operationalizing Algorithmic Work Exposure for Occupational Safety and Health**

## Executive summary

Paper 1 will examine algorithmic changes to worker-facing work conditions as occupational exposures. It will connect algorithmic functions, exposure definitions and measurement, pathways and worker OSH outcomes, and empirically evaluated controls. The proposed scholarly products are a source-linked exposure taxonomy, evidence map, measurement framework, and prioritized questions for later Delphi and experimental studies.

The original five questions are now four: RQ1 exposure characteristics; RQ2 operationalization and measurement; RQ3 integrated pathways and empirical OSH support; RQ4 evaluated controls. ## 1. Purpose and strategic role

The review will identify which algorithmic work characteristics can be defined as measurable occupational exposures, how those characteristics have been operationalized, what worker responses and OSH outcomes have been investigated, and which modifiable controls have been evaluated. It provides an empirical foundation for a proposed future NIOSH R01 on algorithmic hygiene: measurable exposure dimensions and work/software controls that could be tested in subsequent studies.

The organizing connection is:

`algorithmic function -> experienced work condition/exposure -> measurement and time window -> proposed/tested pathway -> worker response or OSH outcome -> evaluated control`.

Each connection must remain traceable to a study and report. A study need not measure the whole chain to contribute evidence. Missing measures or untested links are recorded as gaps. Conventional employment, platform/gig work and HRC/work-task experiments remain distinct contexts rather than presumed interchangeable settings.

## 2. Scientific gap and contribution design

The proposed review addresses four questions about the literature: whether algorithmic work conditions have explicit occupational exposure definitions; whether their units, intensity/frequency/variability and observation windows are specified; how they relate to worker safety and health; and which controls have empirical human evaluation. The extent of these gaps is a hypothesis for the review to test, rather than a completed finding.

Planned contributions are:

- A source-supported taxonomy separating system functions from conditions actually experienced by workers.
- A measurement matrix specifying construct, unit, dose/intensity/frequency, temporal window, source, and reported reliability/validity.
- A linked pathway/outcome/design map that separates proposed mechanisms, empirical associations, temporal evidence and tested mechanisms.
- A control-evaluation matrix separating implemented/evaluated human controls from proposals or technical-only simulations.
- A candidate measurement framework and source-linked priorities for later Delphi, dose-response and intervention research.

Evidence density describes how much eligible literature has been found. It does not independently establish measurement quality, causal support, absence of harm, or preventive effectiveness.

## 3. Four research questions and evaluation

### RQ1: exposure characteristics

Which algorithmic functions and worker-facing work characteristics have been studied as occupational exposures, and how are the exposure dimensions defined?

**Product:** function-to-exposure taxonomy and operational definitions, preserving event/task/shift/worker units and occupational context. **Owner:** RQ1_LEAD.

### RQ2: operationalization and measurement

How have exposures been measured in terms of unit, dose/intensity, frequency, variability, time window, reversibility, and worker control, and what measurement properties and gaps are reported?

**Product:** self-report/log/observation/manipulation matrix, validation-gap synthesis and candidate measurement framework. **Owner:** RQ2_LEAD.

### RQ3: pathways and OSH evidence

Through which proposed or tested cognitive, psychosocial, ergonomic, physical-safety, or organizational pathways are exposures linked to worker OSH outcomes, with what empirical support, limitations, and evidence gaps?

**Product:** linked pathway/outcome/design/support and gap map. **Owner:** RQ3_LEAD. RQ3a retains pathway coding; RQ3b retains outcome and empirical-support coding. These are subdomains of one headline RQ.

### RQ4: modifiable controls

Which software/work-design controls have been empirically implemented or evaluated, at what evaluation level, and with what worker response or OSH outcomes?

**Product:** human control-evaluation matrix plus a separate table of background proposals and later candidates. **Owner:** RQ4_LEAD.

The merger improves presentation by joining pathway and outcome support, while preserving all original content. It does not reduce the independent screening queue, remove RQ3a/RQ3b coding, or strengthen causal inference. RQ3's breadth is managed through distinct fields for mediator measurement, mechanism testing, temporal order, outcome role, effect estimate and design limitations. One eligible study can answer several RQs; their counts must not be added as independent studies. See the [legacy crosswalk](../tracking/rq_crosswalk.csv).

## 4. Review scope, eligibility and units

### 4.1 Working PCC and evidence requirement

**Population:** human workers or participants undertaking a credible simulation of work tasks. **Concept:** algorithmic/AI/adaptive decisions that change a worker-facing condition through allocation/reallocation, schedule/pace, monitoring, evaluation, reward/penalty, control or related automation. **Context:** conventional employment, platform/gig work, and human work-task/HRC experiments, with setting and sector coded explicitly.

Core studies provide original empirical evidence defining, measuring or manipulating the relevant condition and report a worker response, OSH-related outcome, exposure measurement property, or relevant human control evidence. Quantitative, qualitative, mixed-method and human experimental evidence may qualify under the signed rule. Exposure measurement studies need not demonstrate an injury effect. Relevant HRC can qualify without using the phrase algorithmic management.

Priority exposure dimensions include assignment/reassignment frequency; pace/intensity and temporal variability; authority/discretion; reversibility/override; predictability/advance notice; monitoring/feedback intensity; and adaptation frequency. They are coding prompts, not a predetermined final taxonomy.

Priority responses/endpoints include workload, fatigue, stress, situation awareness, autonomy/control, trust/reliance, physical load, ergonomic indicators, error/omission, unsafe continuation, hazard-response/near-miss proxies, and injury/illness when measured. Record each variable's actual role as exposure, mediator, worker state, behavioral proxy or injury/illness endpoint. Do not equate these endpoint types.

### 4.2 Core exclusions and separate background sources

Exclude robot-only route/collision/throughput optimization without relevant human work evidence; consumer/patient-only AI without a work-task analogue; general ethics/technology commentary or macro employment forecasts without the relevant empirical exposure link; and non-empirical reviews, editorials, protocols or guidelines from the core original-study synthesis. Relevant reviews/guidelines remain in a separate comparison/background table. Proposals and technical simulations can inform candidate controls but cannot establish human OSH effectiveness.

Missing abstracts and absent keyword/RQ hints are not exclusion criteria. Ambiguous records advance for fuller assessment under the frozen rule. Language restrictions, conference abstract/full-paper treatment, preprints/grey literature and full-text-unavailable procedures remain unsigned decisions. The detailed [collection criteria](COLLECTION_AND_ELIGIBILITY_CRITERIA.md) and [decision table](../tracking/collection_decisions.csv) govern approval.

### 4.3 Record, report and study

Use `record_id` for retrieval/screening units, `report_id` for full-text documents and `study_id` for underlying investigations. Link multiple reports of the same study; retain original effect-estimate analysis units and repeated-measure structure. Events, shifts, outcomes, effects, reports and multiple RQ contributions do not create additional independent studies.

## 8. Independent reviewer design and screening

Two trained paid independent non-author reviewers will screen the same complete approved queue and all full-text reports. They do not generate hypotheses, develop the final framework or interpret final results. Confirm funding, availability, research-method competence and a written competency check before formal work. The original source assigns lead-author governance and unresolved adjudication to Nam/Staley; actual acceptance and roles remain to be confirmed. A separate additional adjudicator may be appointed.

DATA supplies identical versioned metadata in separate reviewer packets, verifies IDs/count/hash and preserves each original. Reviewers cannot see each other's judgments until both are locked. Allowed screening decisions are include, exclude and uncertain. Each exclusion requires a principal standardized reason; uncertainty advances under the signed rule. Missing abstracts are recovered with source/failure logging and do not trigger automatic exclusion.

After locked decisions, calculate pre-consensus statistics, discuss disagreements, and refer unresolved cases to ADJ. Preserve original judgments, reconciliation, reason/date and any affected-field recoding. Full-text unavailability is used only after the approved library/open-copy/authorized-contact procedure and its time window have been documented; this draft does not authorize sending author requests.

Training uses the existing mixed 93-record ID pool and positive/negative/borderline examples. The separate 50-ID calibration manifest was randomly drawn from the remaining queue, in two disjoint rounds of 25. It prepares samples only; it does not establish training completion, competency or reliability.

## 9. Calibration, reliability and codebook governance

### 9.1 Title/abstract and full-text gates

For title/abstract round 1, both reviewers independently assess 25 random records. Record disagreement type, revise examples/codebook and retrain as needed. Round 2 uses 25 new random records. The source project targets are raw agreement **>=85%** and Gwet's AC1 **>=0.80**, with Cohen's kappa also reported. If unmet, repeat on fresh samples; review substantive errors even when numeric targets pass. Previous judgments affected by codebook changes must be independently repeated under the frozen version.

Full-text calibration uses **10-15 reports**, including borderline cases, with source targets raw agreement **>=90%** and AC1 **>=0.80**, plus kappa. After its gate, every advanced report receives dual independent eligibility assessment and a recorded principal exclusion reason when excluded.

### 9.2 Field tiers and interpretive reliability

Descriptive fields are extracted once and independently verified against the source for **100%** of records. Core interpretive fields are **100% independently double-coded**. Keep source locators, verification and a separate consensus table.

Core reliability is calculated from original independent codes before discussion/adjudication: percent agreement, Krippendorff's alpha with 95% confidence intervals, prespecified AC1/AC2 for imbalanced nominal/ordinal categories, and appropriate supplementary kappa. Freeze the primary coefficient, imbalance rule, CI method, multi-label treatment and independent unit before examining results. A coefficient is not selected because it passes.

The source target is **>=0.80**. Values **0.67-0.79** trigger refinement and independent recoding of affected fields; values **<0.67** trigger reconsideration of category structure. CI calculation must respect clustering within study/report units; repeated links/effects do not become independent reliability observations. Retain missingness and denominators in the stage/field reliability table.

Archive codebook versions, changes, rationale and affected records. Scientific criteria changes require an amendment and re-screening decision; interpretive-category changes require independent recoding of all affected prior studies. Blank reviewer files cannot generate reliability results. The [reliability plan](../tracking/reliability_plan.csv) and [SOP](SCREENING_AND_EXTRACTION_SOP.md) are implementation references.

## 11. Analysis and measurement-framework development

The primary synthesis is descriptive, relational and narrative. Meta-analysis is not the primary plan; it requires a separate documented compatibility assessment of definitions, designs, units, estimands and effect measures. Do not pool heterogeneous outcomes solely because they share a label.

RQ1 produces function-to-exposure definitions and context distributions. RQ2 maps constructs to self-report/log/observation/manipulation approaches, metrics/time windows and validation gaps. RQ3 joins exposures, pathways, outcomes and design support with effect/temporal/confounding detail. RQ4 organizes human control evaluations, endpoint support and implementation gaps, separately from future candidates.

Use unique `study_id` denominators in main synthesis, report the universe and valid-field denominators for each table, and retain missingness. Evidence-density heatmaps show counts rather than effect direction or certainty. Link estimates to reports and their original units; distinguish cross-sectional associations, prospective exposure-response evidence, mediation tests and experimental control effects. An empty cell means no eligible evidence identified under the completed search/criteria, not proof that a relationship or effect does not exist.

RQ2_LEAD coordinates a source-linked candidate measurement framework, with RQ1 construct definitions, RQ3 response/support gaps and RQ4 modifiable controls. For each candidate, specify definition, measurable unit/time/dose, feasible measurement/manipulation, validation evidence/gaps and controls. PI approves the integrated interpretation. Candidate future manipulations are proposals requiring validation and experimental design.

They do not judge study eligibility. Packet validators, disagreement tables, reliability calculations, approved-code relational maps and final PRISMA count scripts are planned implementations requiring the signed schema and actual human decisions.

Permitted automation includes integrity checks, duplicate candidates, metadata/abstract retrieval with provenance, administrative packet checks, reading-order assistance and deterministic summaries of human-approved codes. Record tool/version, input/hash, date, command, output and human check. An AI suggestion is a prompt for review, not evidence.

Human reviewers and authors retain final screening, exposure/pathway/outcome/control classification, source quotation verification, evidence interpretation and review conclusions. No AI-based stopping rule leaves records unscreened. Do not generate numerical findings from blank files or infer validated measurement/causation/prevention from routing hints. Disclose material workflow assistance according to journal policy at submission. This working plan was drafted with AI document/coordination assistance; it supplies no included-study decisions, source-paper codes or completed review results.
