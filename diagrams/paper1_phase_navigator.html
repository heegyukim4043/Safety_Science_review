Handoff rules

Individual reviewed PRs may merge during a phase. The whole-phase checkpoint stays pending until all required outputs, evidence and approvals are complete.

P6A uses one agreed G5 evidence freeze. Its four round-1 branches and review establish midpoint G6A. Fresh P6B branches and second evaluation establish G6 before P7 starts. RQ3 combines pathways and OSH evidence; RQ4 covers evaluated controls.

Automation supports hashes, IDs, count checks, packet preparation and approved-code summaries. Humans decide eligibility, resolve coding disagreements, interpret evidence and approve gates.
