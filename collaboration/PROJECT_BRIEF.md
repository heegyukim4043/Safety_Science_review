# Paper 1: proposed collaboration

## 1. Research plan and questions

The proposed study is a scoping review and evidence map of algorithmic work conditions as occupational exposures. It examines how algorithms change experienced work conditions, how exposures are measured, their pathways to worker occupational safety and health (OSH), and evaluated software/work-design controls.

The four proposed questions cover **RQ1 exposure characteristics**, **RQ2 operationalization and measurement**, **RQ3 pathways and worker OSH evidence**, and **RQ4 evaluated controls**. RQ3 retains distinct pathway and outcome/support fields. We propose one accountable analytical lead per RQ, using a common approved evidence set and cross-review.

## 2. Survey preparation

- **Databases:** PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library.
- **Working publication period:** 2015-2026. Many original queries specify 1 January 2015 through 2 October 2026; the final cutoff and update plan require agreement. Actual search execution dates are not yet verified.
- **Proposed inclusion:** original empirical human-worker or credible work-task studies that define, measure or manipulate an algorithmically changed work condition and report relevant worker response/OSH, measurement-property or human-control evidence.
- **Proposed exclusions/separate background:** robot-only technical optimization, consumer/patient-only AI without a work-task analogue, unrelated/general non-empirical discussion, and technical-only control simulations. Relevant reviews/protocols remain separately identifiable for background/comparison. Language and publication-type policies require confirmation.
- **Process:** combine algorithmic management/control and HRC concepts with relevant OSH, measurement or control terms; translate queries for each platform; preserve exports; normalize metadata; provisionally group duplicates and route records to overlapping RQ pools.
- **Progress:** 56 exports, 10,858 retrieval rows, 6,822 provisional unique records and a 6,592-record working-year queue. Exports exist for 25 of 42 planned queries. Human eligibility screening and agreed extraction remain pending.

Please review the scope, search coverage and proposed criteria, and suggest any revisions or missing sources.

## 3. Collaboration and phases

The repository will support collaborator access, task branches, reviewed PRs to `main`, and phase-based acceptance. Common protocol/search/calibration/screening/coding work precedes RQ analysis. Four RQ round-1 inventories are reviewed at midpoint G6A; fresh round-2 branches complete revised synthesis for second evaluation G6, followed by integration and reporting.

The five planned layers are JBI review conduct, PRISMA-ScR review reporting, PRISMA-S search reporting, independent PRESS review and OSF preregistration. PRESS and registration are pending. Prior discovery searches will be disclosed.
