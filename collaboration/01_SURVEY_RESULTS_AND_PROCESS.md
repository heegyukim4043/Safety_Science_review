## B. Reconstructable process

1. Platform-specific searches were collected using the supplied query strategy. The [search log](../tracking/search_log.csv) preserves exact input strings, fields/limits, search IDs, export status and unresolved information. It is the shareable representation of the supplied site-specific search instructions.
2. Preserve original exports and SHA-256 hashes using the [manifest](../tracking/raw_export_manifest.csv).
3. Normalize bibliographic fields and provisionally group repeated retrievals using identifiers/title-year information, while retaining source links and ambiguous cases.
4. Prepare the working 2015-2026 partition and overlapping keyword-based RQ routing; retain records with missing abstracts or no RQ hint.
5. Prepare separate blank reviewer packets and training/calibration IDs. No human judgments are generated by this preparation.
6. Complete provenance/duplicate QA, sign the protocol/criteria, obtain search peer review and registration, then conduct calibrated independent screening. Final flow counts follow actual decisions and record/report/study linkage.

## D. Boundaries and remaining gaps

Working core eligibility concerns human workers or credible work-task simulations, an algorithmically changed worker-facing condition, and relevant original empirical worker response/OSH, exposure-measurement or human control evidence. HRC can qualify without the phrase algorithmic management. Robot-only optimization, consumer/patient-only AI, non-empirical reviews/protocols and technical-only control simulations are outside the core study synthesis; relevant background sources remain separately identifiable.

These rules have not been applied through completed independent screening. Language, source types, HRC borderline cases and unavailable-full-text procedures require signed decisions in the [criteria register](../tracking/collection_decisions.csv).

- 3,034 queue records have no abstract; 1,015 have no RQ hint. Neither condition automatically excludes a record.
- Five conflicting-DOI groups and nine of 43 priority references without DOI linkage need manual resolution.
- RQ candidate pools overlap: RQ1 5,079; RQ2 1,105; RQ3 2,958; RQ4 905. These describe routing/workload, not evidence quantity or quality.
- Novelty remains a proposition to evaluate. Count Chen's 167-paper review once; compare adjacent reviews and keep protocols distinguishable from completed reviews. Do not infer unsupported worker-response categories from an abstract.
