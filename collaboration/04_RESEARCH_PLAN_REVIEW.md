# 4. Research plan review and approval agenda

Read the [full English research plan](../docs/RESEARCH_PLAN.md) as the canonical scientific proposal. This briefing specifies what collaborators should confirm; it does not duplicate or replace the plan.

## Proposed design

**Study:** a scoping review and evidence map of algorithmic work conditions as occupational exposures. **Objective:** connect functions, experienced exposure definitions/measurement, proposed/tested pathways, worker OSH outcomes and empirically evaluated controls. **Context:** conventional employment, platform/gig work and human work-task/HRC settings, coded separately. The four RQs and source-linked products are in the [RQ briefing](02_RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md).

**Proposed contribution:** a function-to-exposure taxonomy, measurement matrix, pathway/outcome/support map, human control-evaluation matrix, and integrated measurement framework. The claim of a unique cross-analysis remains conditional on completed searches and full-text comparison with adjacent reviews. Later Delphi and experimental/dose-response/intervention studies are separate projects, informed by this review rather than performed within it.

## Five methodological layers

| Layer | Commitment to approve | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| JBI | Scope/protocol, common selection/charting and synthesis design | Approved protocol, human decision records, source-linked evidence and accepted synthesis |
| PRISMA-ScR | Review reporting from early records to final manuscript | Final item-location checklist, reconciled selection flow and study/result tables |
| PRISMA-S | Reproducible search reporting and explicit limitations | Exact strategies/platforms/dates/limits/counts, update/dedup records and checklist |
| PRESS | Independent review before final/update strategy execution | Dated query/comments/responses and revised strategy; qualified independent reviewer |
| OSF | Formal prospective protocol registration before screening | Actual registration identifier/date/version, prior-search disclosure and amendments |

See the [methodology-to-phase guide](../docs/METHODOLOGY_AND_PHASE_CROSSWALK.md). PRESS and OSF are not documented as completed. The existing survey is prior discovery/collection and must be disclosed at registration.

## Procedural decisions

- Approve PCC, publication window/cutoff, source/language/HRC boundaries, report/study linkage and unavailable-full-text treatment in the [collection register](../tracking/collection_decisions.csv).
- Approve two independent paid non-author reviewers for the same full queue, training/competency, separate random 25+25 title/abstract calibration and separate 10-15 full-text calibration. Confirm the adjudication arrangement, funding and hours.
- Approve 100% independent core double coding, descriptive extraction with 100% second-person verification, primary reliability statistic/CI/multi-label/imbalance rules and recoding criteria before actual coding. These are project choices rather than universal requirements of the five layers.
- Decide whether design-appropriate formal critical appraisal serves the review objective; no universal quality score, GRADE assessment or quality-based exclusion rule has been adopted.
- Approve AI/admin assistance boundaries and document actual use. Humans decide eligibility, scientific codes, evidence interpretation and effectiveness claims.
- Approve descriptive matrices, study-linked relational maps and narrative synthesis as the primary analyses. Meta-analysis would require a separate compatibility/feasibility decision. Operationalization, validity, causal support and evidence density remain separate.

## Execution and final deliverables

Approve the [shared phases and two RQ rounds](03_WORK_ALLOCATION_AND_PHASES.md): P1-P5 common preparation -> G5 -> P6A inventories -> midpoint G6A -> P6B final synthesis -> second evaluation G6 -> P7 integration/G7 -> P8 reporting/G8. Named roles and observed throughput determine the working schedule. Existing target dates require capacity-based review after calibration.

The final package should contain the manuscript, agreed study descriptors and RQ tables, reconciled flow, source-linked evidence map, measurement framework, search/codebook/reliability/amendment supplements and reproducible analysis/release records. The team still needs to agree a target journal, authorship/contribution arrangement, data access and release approach; none is assigned by this packet.
