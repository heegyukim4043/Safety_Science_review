# 2. Four research questions and requested confirmation

**RQ version: four_rq_v0.2; pending signed protocol acceptance.**

## Objective and organizing link

Identify algorithmic changes to worker-facing work conditions as occupational exposures; characterize their definition/measurement, pathways to worker occupational safety and health (OSH), and evaluated modifiable controls.

`algorithmic function -> experienced work condition/exposure -> measurement/time window -> pathway -> worker response/OSH outcome -> evaluated control`

Each link is source-traceable. A study can contribute part of the chain and several RQs. Conventional employment, platform/gig work and HRC/work-task experiments remain identifiable contexts.

## Questions, fields and products

| RQ | Exact working question | Principal fields / final product |
|---|---|---|
| **RQ1: exposure characteristics** | Which algorithmic functions and worker-facing work characteristics have been studied as occupational exposures, and how are the exposure dimensions defined? | Function, experienced condition, construct definition, unit, authority, predictability/reversibility; function-to-exposure taxonomy |
| **RQ2: operationalization and measurement** | How have exposures been measured in terms of unit, dose/intensity, frequency, variability, time window, reversibility, and worker control, and what measurement properties and gaps are reported? | Source, dose/time, self-report/log/observation/manipulation, reported reliability/validity; measurement matrix and candidate framework |
| **RQ3: pathways and OSH evidence** | Through which proposed or tested cognitive, psychosocial, ergonomic, physical-safety, or organizational pathways are exposures linked to worker OSH outcomes, with what empirical support, limitations, and evidence gaps? | RQ3a mechanism proposed/measured/tested; RQ3b outcome role, estimate, design, temporal order/confounding; linked pathway/outcome/support/gap map |
| **RQ4: modifiable controls** | Which software/work-design controls have been empirically implemented or evaluated, at what evaluation level, and with what worker response or OSH outcomes? | Control type, implementation/evaluation level, comparator, fidelity, worker outcome and limitations; human control-evaluation matrix plus separate proposals |

## Revision and interpretation rules

- Legacy RQ1/RQ2 remain current RQ1/RQ2. Legacy RQ3/RQ4 combine into current RQ3 with distinct RQ3a/RQ3b coding fields. Legacy RQ5 becomes current RQ4. See the [crosswalk](../tracking/rq_crosswalk.csv).
- RQ1 distinguishes system functions from experienced work conditions. RQ2 distinguishes measurement operationalization from validation. RQ3 distinguishes proposed pathways, associations and stronger empirical support. RQ4 distinguishes proposals, implementation and evaluated outcomes.
- The merger preserves original pathway/outcome content. It does not reduce the screening queue or establish causal evidence. No-hint and missing-abstract records still receive screening.
- Keep record/report/study/effect units separate. Do not add overlapping RQ counts or treat multiple effects/reports as additional independent studies.
- Keyword responsibilities support coverage and expertise. RQ leads analyze an agreed common corpus after G5; two independent reviewers screen the same full queue.
