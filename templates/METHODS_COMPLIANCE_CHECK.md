## Framework-specific checks

- JBI: approved scope/protocol, selection, charting and planned synthesis traceability.
- PRISMA-ScR: completed reporting checklist with manuscript/supplement locations and reconciled study-selection flow.
- PRISMA-S: exact histories, platforms, dates, filters, supplementary/update searches, deduplication and counts; complete final checklist locations.
- PRESS: independent reviewer identity, dated strategy/comments, responses, revised query and execution references.
- OSF: formal registration identifier/date, exact protocol version, prior-work disclosure, amendment/deviation reconciliation.
