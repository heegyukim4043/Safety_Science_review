# Paper 1: Algorithmic Work Exposure and Occupational Safety and Health

**Study:** scoping review, evidence map and measurement framework.  
**Team review version:** 12 October 2026 | Four RQs v0.2 | Scientific plan v0.3 | Execution v0.5.

## Research objective

Identify how algorithmic decisions change worker-facing work conditions, how these conditions are defined and measured as occupational exposures, their links to worker safety and health (OSH), and empirically evaluated controls. Conventional employment, platform/gig work and human-robot collaboration are examined as distinct contexts.

## 1. Survey results and process

Completed preparation includes source inventories, normalization, provisional duplicate grouping and overlapping RQ routing. Human eligibility screening and agreed extraction remain pending. Search dates, export completeness, unavailable-source decisions and the final period/update plan need confirmation. These are preparation counts, not final included-study or PRISMA counts.

## 3. Work allocation and phases

**Shared work:** PI, SEARCH, DATA, ADJ and two independent reviewers handle protocol/search QA, screening, coding and the agreed evidence freeze. Both reviewers screen the same complete queue. **RQ work:** four accountable leads analyze that common approved evidence and cross-review RQ1/RQ2 and RQ3/RQ4, with DATA checks.

| Phase | Intermediate goal |
|---|---|
| P1-P5 | Approve protocol/search, calibrate reviewers, complete screening/full-text review and freeze agreed extraction at G5 |
| P6A | Four RQ inventories, classifications and descriptive products; midpoint review at G6A |
| P6B | Resolve actions and complete linked synthesis; second evaluation at G6 |
| P7 | Integrate evidence map, measurement framework and manuscript at G7 |
| P8 | Complete reporting, reproducibility and final release review at G8 |

RQ round-1 branches start from G5; fresh round-2 branches start from G6A. All PRs target `main`; the next stage starts after its required approval. Automate inventories, integrity checks and approved-code summaries; humans decide eligibility, interpretation and scientific acceptance.
